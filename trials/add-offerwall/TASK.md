Add the Moonetix offerwall to Coinbarn so users can earn coins by completing offers. We have a Moonetix account: the app id is in `MOONETIX_APP_ID`, the postback secret key in `MOONETIX_POSTBACK_KEY` and the link hash salt in `MOONETIX_LINK_SALT`. In the Moonetix dashboard we set the postback URL to `https://api.coinbarn.app/postback/moonetix?user={user_id}&tx={transaction_id}&event={event_id}&status={status}&reward={reward}&payout={payout}&hash={hash}`, and the reward is already in coins (rate and share are set there).

The front end needs `GET /api/offerwall` to return `{"url": ...}`, the wall link for the signed-in user. Offer coins can be spent right away, but they may only be cashed out (`POST /api/payouts`, `GET /api/payouts` -> `withdrawable`) once they are older than the hold in `OFFER_HOLD_SECONDS` (default 30 days). Balances stay in `GET /api/me`, and `GET /admin/reconcile` must keep reporting no mismatches.
