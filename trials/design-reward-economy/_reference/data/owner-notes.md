# Numbers and decisions (from the owner, 2026-11-14)

Measured over the last three finished months (August to October 2026), monthly averages:

- Revenue the networks credited us, before reversals: $12,000 a month.
- Active users: 3,000 a month. New sign-ups: 1,800 a month.
- Reversals: 7% of revenue is taken back by the networks. Of all reversals, 30% arrive within 7 days of the credit, 55% within 14 days, 85% within 30, 94% within 45, 98% within 60 and all of them within 90 days.
- When a reversal arrives after the coins were cashed out, we still recover about 25% of it from the user's later earnings.
- Gift card fees (Giftly): 2% of the amount plus $0.25 per payout. We pay them, not the user.
- Average payout: $11. About 92% of all coins credited end up cashed out.
- Smallest offer payout we see: $0.05.
- Other costs that grow with revenue (servers, support tools): 3% of revenue. Fixed costs: $1,200 a month.
- Users hold 9,000,000 coins right now ($9,000 at today's rate). Cash reserve: $15,000. The networks owe us $14,000 that they have confirmed but not paid yet. They pay about 40 days after a conversion.
- About 30% of active users check in on an average day.
- Referral commission is paid on about 20% of all offer earnings (the earnings of invited users).

Owner decisions:

- Keep at least a 15% margin.
- Catch at least 90% of reversals before coins can be cashed out.
- An average active user must be able to reach the payout minimum within 30 days.
- Bonuses may cost at most 10% of the revenue we keep.
- Keep the sign-up bonus, the daily check-in and the referral commission, but the amounts are up to you.
- Coins users already hold keep their dollar value.
