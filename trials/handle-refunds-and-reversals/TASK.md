Refunds and chargebacks don't touch coins in Coinbarn today. When we refund a coin pack in Stripe, or a buyer disputes the charge with their bank, they keep the coins. Rewardly also tells us when an advertiser takes a conversion back (the same postback to `/postback/rewardly` with the same `tx` and `status=2`), and we ignore that too. Please make refunds, disputes and offer reversals take the coins back.

In the Stripe dashboard we've enabled `charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created` and `charge.dispute.closed` for our existing `POST /webhooks/stripe` endpoint, next to the Checkout events it already handles. Our charges carry the order id in `metadata.order_id`. Support sometimes refunds only part of an order, and a partial refund should take back the matching share of the pack's coins. Balances are read from `GET /api/me` (`balance`), and `GET /admin/reconcile` must keep reporting no mismatches.
