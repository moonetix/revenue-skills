Add a referral program to Coinbarn. Every user gets an invite code, shown as `ref_code` in `GET /api/me`, and a friend signs up with it by sending `{"ref": "<code>"}` to `POST /api/signup`. The referrer earns a 500-coin bonus once the friend has bought or earned at least 500 coins: Stripe purchases and Rewardly offers count, promo codes and coins received from other users don't. If a refund or an offer reversal later takes the friend back below 500, the bonus goes back too.

Bonus coins can only be spent in the app, never cashed out, so pay the bonus as soon as the friend qualifies, without a waiting period. Pay at most 20 bonuses per referrer in any 30 days; anything above that waits for us to review. People must not be able to collect bonuses by inviting a second account of their own. `GET /api/referrals` should show the signed-in user their referrals as `{"referrals": [...]}`, one entry per invited account with its state and join date, but not the friend's email address. Balances stay in `GET /api/me`, and `GET /admin/reconcile` must keep reporting no mismatches.
