Add cash-out to Coinbarn: users should be able to turn coins they earned from Rewardly offers into Giftly gift cards. The Giftly API is described in `docs/giftly-api.md`; its URL and key come from the `GIFTLY_API_URL` and `GIFTLY_API_KEY` environment variables, and `tools/giftly-sandbox.mjs` is their sandbox. 1,000 coins are worth $1.00.

The rules: at least 5,000 coins per cash-out, at most 50,000 coins and 3 cash-outs per user in any 24 hours. Only coins earned from offers can be cashed out (bought, promo, referral and gifted coins are for spending), and only once they are older than the hold in `PAYOUT_HOLD_SECONDS` (default 7 days). No manual review for now: a valid request is sent to Giftly right away.

API: `POST /api/payouts` with `{"amount": <coins>}` and an optional `Idempotency-Key` header (a retry with the same key must return the same payout and never pay twice) answers 2xx with `{"payout_id", "status"}`. `GET /api/payouts` returns `{"withdrawable": <coins>, "payouts": [{"id", "coins", "cents", "status"}]}`, where `status` is `paid` once Giftly issued the card, `failed` when Giftly refused it (the coins go back), and anything else while we don't know yet. Support needs `POST /admin/payouts/resolve` (with `X-Admin-Key`) to settle payouts whose outcome is still unknown. Balances stay in `GET /api/me`, and `GET /admin/reconcile` must keep reporting no mismatches.
