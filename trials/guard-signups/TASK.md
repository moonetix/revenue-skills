We want to give every new Coinbarn account a 100-coin welcome bonus and start running promo codes, without both being farmed by fake and duplicate accounts. Accounts that look fine should get the bonus at sign-up (`POST /api/signup`). Suspicious ones, for example sign-ups from cloud or datacenter addresses (the ranges we know are in `data/cloud-ranges.txt`), still get a working account, but their bonus waits until their first purchase or first offer earning arrives. Refuse duplicate accounts, including the Gmail dot and plus tricks, and throwaway email addresses (our list is `data/disposable-domains.txt`).

Promo codes: add `POST /api/promo` with `{"code": "..."}`. `WELCOME100` gives 100 coins and may be redeemed at most 10,000 times in total, `LAUNCH5` gives 250 coins and only to the first 5 accounts. Each code works once per account. We don't verify email addresses yet, so don't make anything depend on that. The app runs behind our reverse proxy on 127.0.0.1, which appends the client's address to `X-Forwarded-For`. Balances stay in `GET /api/me`, and `GET /admin/reconcile` must keep reporting no mismatches.
