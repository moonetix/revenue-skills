We're launching Coinbarn Plus, a $4.99 monthly subscription on Stripe Billing, and it isn't wired up yet. `POST /api/plus/checkout` creates a pending checkout and sends the user to Stripe Checkout in subscription mode, but nothing ever gives anyone Plus or takes it away. `GET /api/plus/perk` is the Plus-only route: it must answer 200 while the user has Plus, and 402 or 403 when they don't. Plus members also get 300 coins for every month they pay for, shown in `GET /api/me` -> `balance`.

Stripe calls our existing `POST /webhooks/stripe` (signing secret in `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`). For it we've enabled `customer.subscription.created`, `customer.subscription.updated`, `customer.subscription.deleted`, `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `charge.refunded` and `charge.dispute.created`, on API version `2026-10-28.endive`. Every Plus subscription carries our checkout id in `metadata.checkout_id`. The Stripe API is at `STRIPE_API_URL` with the key in `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`; the calls we use are in `docs/stripe-api.md`, and `tools/stripe-sandbox.mjs` stands in for Stripe locally.

How Plus should work: it starts when the first payment goes through. If a renewal payment fails, Stripe retries for a while; the user keeps Plus for `PLUS_GRACE_SECONDS` (default 3 days) after the end of the last month they paid for, and after that loses it until a payment succeeds. Someone who cancels keeps Plus until the end of the month they paid for; a subscription that is ended immediately instead (support sometimes does that in the Stripe dashboard) ends Plus right away. If we refund a Plus payment, or the customer disputes it with their bank, Plus ends right away. Support also needs `POST /admin/plus/sync` (with `X-Admin-Key`) that compares who has Plus in our database with Stripe's list of subscriptions and fixes any difference, for the times a webhook gets lost. Keep everything else working, and `GET /admin/reconcile` must keep reporting no mismatches.
